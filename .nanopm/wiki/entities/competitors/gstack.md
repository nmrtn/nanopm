## Summary
gstack is an engineering-layer skill pack that complements rather than competes with nanopm. It targets the same AI-coding-agent ecosystem with a "Software Factory" framing, and has reached feature parity on multi-platform delivery while diverging from nanopm on telemetry approach.

## What we know
**Versioning cadence**
v1.42.2.0 → v1.55.1.0 in 12 days (~1 release/day cadence).
- "v1.42.2.0 → v1.55.1.0 in 12 days (13 minor versions, ~1/day cadence)" — competitors.md, 2026-07-01

**Positioning shift**
Tagline evolved from engineering-team to factory framing.
- "Tagline shifted from 'Engineering Team' to 'Software Factory'" — competitors.md, 2026-07-01

Ships transparent telemetry by default with explicit opt-in screen; regression-test pinned.

**Platform scope**

- "Platform list expanded: Codex, OpenCode, Cursor, Factory Droid, Slate, Kiro, Hermes, GBrain. Windows 11 (WSL/Git Bash) now explicit." — competitors.md, 2026-07-01

**Telemetry divergence from nanopm**
nanopm dropped telemetry as pre-PMF over-engineering (v0.6.0); gstack shipped it with explicit consent (v1.55). Both defensible.
