# DevFlow — pricing (fetched 2026-07-02)
Ticket-based / done-for-you model (agents deliver "tickets", not per-seat):
- Starter: $2,500/mo — 5 tickets, 1 agent, 1 revision/ticket, 48h response.
- Growth: $6,000/mo — 15 tickets, 3 parallel agents, 3 revisions/ticket, 24h response.
- Scale: $10,000/mo — 30 tickets, 20 parallel agents, 10 revisions/ticket, same-day response.
Extra tickets: $500 (Starter) / $400 (Growth) / $350 (Scale) each.
Enterprise: custom volume plans + dedicated support.
Billing: month-to-month, no long contract, pause up to 2 months/yr.
