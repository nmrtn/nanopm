## Strategic implications

**DevFlow** validates nanopm's core thesis (AI PM + shared memory + spec→build) from a well-funded, on-narrative competitor — and forces the question of *why nanopm's model wins*: local/host-neutral, near-free, builder-in-the-loop, runs in your own agent, vs DevFlow's expensive managed delivery where its agents do the work and the buyer steps out. The concept is no longer unique; the *model and control* are the moat. Track DevFlow closely for any move toward self-serve.

**Aha!** is the market ceiling nanopm approaches from below — no threat today; only relevant if its AI push goes agent/CLI-native rather than staying GUI-bound.
