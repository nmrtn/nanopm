# Productboard Spark — Pricing Page Snapshot
Source: https://www.productboard.com/pricing/
Fetched: 2026-07-01

## Plan Overview

| Plan | Price | Billing | AI Credits | Minimum Users |
|------|-------|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Free | $0 | Annual | 50/month | N/A |
| Plus | $19/maker | Annual | 250/maker/month | N/A |
| Business | $59/maker | Annual | 500/maker/month | 2 makers |
| Enterprise | Custom | Annual | 800/maker/month | 5 makers |

Annual billing = 21% savings vs. monthly.

## Key Feature Differences

**Free:**
- 500 feedback notes
- 25 contributors
- 1 teamspace & 1 Product Portal
- Full Spark access

**Plus** (adds to Free):
- Manual & dynamic customer segments
- Feedback loop closing
- Product usage integrations (Amplitude, Mixpanel)

**Business** (adds to Plus):
- Unlimited feedback notes
- Unlimited teamspaces
- Shared skills library
- 2 Product Portals with customization
- Enhanced platform customization

**Enterprise** (adds to Business):
- Unlimited contributors
- SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning
- Custom roles & permissions
- Salesforce integration
- Live onboarding & customer support
- Full platform customization

## Notes
- Spark (AI) included in all plans, even Free
- "Makers" = full users with edit rights
- "Contributors" = view/comment-only users
