## Summary
DevFlow is the **closest competitor to nanopm on concept** seen so far: "An AI product team, in one workspace" that orchestrates autonomous agents (PM, architect, coder, tester) with **shared context + memory**, running spec → design → code → test → deploy. That's nanopm's exact thesis (an AI PM + compounding memory + build handoff). But the **model is the inverse**: DevFlow is a hosted, done-for-you **agentic delivery service** — you buy "tickets" ($2,500–$10,000/mo) and their agents do the work — whereas nanopm is a local skill pack you run inside *your own* AI coding agent, near-free, terminal-native, with the builder staying in the loop. French infra (built in France, sovereign data), same market/network as nanopm's authors.

## What we know
**Positioning & latest change**
Baseline capture 2026-07-02 — first time tracked; no diff yet.

- [E] Ticket-based done-for-you pricing (their agents deliver): Starter $2,500/mo (5 tickets, 1 agent), Growth $6,000/mo (15 tickets, 3 agents), Scale $10,000/mo (30 tickets, 20 agents). — pricing, 2026-07-02

**Strengths**
- [E] Directly nails the "AI product team" narrative with a shared-memory multi-agent workspace and broad native integrations (GitHub, Linear, Jira, Notion, Figma, Slack, Sentry). — homepage, 2026-07-02
- [E] Outcome/managed model removes setup burden — the buyer submits tickets and receives delivered work, with revision SLAs and parallel agents. — pricing, 2026-07-02
- [A] European/sovereign-data positioning is a real differentiator for EU teams wary of US SaaS. (Assumed — stated as "built in France," relevance inferred.)

**Weaknesses**
- [E] Expensive and hands-off: $2,500–$10,000/mo for 5–30 tickets, extra tickets $350–$500 each — a services-grade price, not a self-serve tool. — pricing, 2026-07-02
- [A] The done-for-you model takes the builder OUT of the loop (agents deliver tickets) — the opposite of a tool that augments the builder's own agent; weak fit for the hands-on solo builder. (Assumed.)
- [A] No visible self-serve/free tier or public changelog found — likely early-stage, higher-touch. (Assumed — no pricing-free entry or changelog surfaced.)

**Gaps vs nanopm**
- [E] They have a hosted multi-agent workspace with shared memory, delivery SLAs, and a paid ticket business — nanopm does not. — homepage/pricing, 2026-07-02
- [A] nanopm is host-neutral and runs inside the builder's existing agent (Claude/Vibe/Codex), local-first, near-free, with typed compounding state and an adversarial gate, keeping the builder in control — DevFlow (a managed platform where its agents deliver) does not. (Assumed — nanopm side stated explicitly; not evidenced by DevFlow's pages.)

## Watch
The most important competitor to monitor. Signals that would raise the threat: a **cheaper self-serve / free tier** (moving from managed-service into nanopm's tool lane), the "AI product team" framing winning mindshare, or **terminal/agent-native** delivery (vs their GUI workspace). Its ticket/outcome pricing is a distinct wedge — track whether that model gains traction vs self-serve.
