## Summary
Productboard Spark is a web-based agentic product system targeting established PM teams (not solo builders). It frames itself as "the first agentic product system" and includes a free tier — both items that overlap with nanopm's category framing and zero-friction positioning, even though the primary user (enterprise PM team) is nanopm's anti-persona.

## What we know
**Category claim**
Launched with the "first agentic product system" label.
- "Launched as 'the first agentic product system.'" — competitors.md, 2026-07-01

**Feature surface**
5 AI workflow areas: Opportunity Discovery (weekly), Feedback Analysis (statistical + citations), Product Specification (delivery-ready specs + codebase analysis via GitHub), Post-Launch Evaluation (Amplitude/Pendo connectors), Release Communications.
- "5 AI workflow areas: Opportunity Discovery (weekly), Feedback Analysis (statistical + citations), Product Specification (delivery-ready specs + codebase analysis via GitHub), Post-Launch Evaluation (Amplitude/Pendo connectors), Release Communications" — competitors.md, 2026-07-01

**Pricing**
Freemium model with AI included in free tier.
- "Pricing: Free ($0, full Spark, 50 AI credits/mo) → Plus ($19/maker) → Business ($59/maker) → Enterprise (custom)" — competitors.md, 2026-07-01

**MCP integrations**
GitHub, Linear, Notion, Confluence, Slack, Amplitude, Gong.
- "MCP integrations include GitHub, Linear, Notion, Confluence, Slack, Amplitude, Gong" — competitors.md, 2026-07-01
