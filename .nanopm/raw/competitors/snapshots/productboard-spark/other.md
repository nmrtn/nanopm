# Productboard Spark — Product Page Snapshot
Source: https://www.productboard.com/product/spark/
Fetched: 2026-07-01

## What It Is
Productboard Spark is an AI agent integrated into Productboard's product management platform, designed to assist product teams with research, analysis, and documentation tasks. Described as "the first agentic product system."

## Key Features (5 main workflow areas)

1. **Opportunity Discovery** — Surfaces potential new products weekly, enriched with market signals, customer segment data, and competitive analysis

2. **Feedback Analysis** — Performs statistical analysis of organizational feedback with citations and trend monitoring

3. **Product Specification** — Generates delivery-ready specs by synthesizing context and performing codebase analysis

4. **Post-Launch Evaluation** — Connects to analytics data sources to measure product performance against original objectives

5. **Release Communications** — Generates release notes, status updates, and enablement materials

## Core Differentiators
- **Persistent context**: Retains knowledge of product, customers, and market over time
- **Traceable outputs**: All recommendations link to source feedback and documents
- **Multiplayer collaboration**: Built for team collaboration with version history and inline editing
- **Purpose-built workflows**: Includes PM best practices rather than generic AI patterns

## Data Connections / Integrations
Amplitude, Pendo, Hex, GitHub, Gong, Linear, Notion, Confluence, Google Drive, Slack, and others via MCP connectors.

## Target Users
Product managers, product leaders, and cross-functional collaborators on product teams (not solo builders).
